Kernel sources: GPL-2.0-only. See src/kernel/LICENSE and
licenses/GPL-2.0-YogaSMC.txt. The VPC command protocol and numeric hotkey mapping
were checked against Linux ideapad-laptop and YogaSMC/ReYogaSMC. The kernel
implementation is restricted to the target machine and commands documented in
README.md. Full corresponding kernel sources are included under src/kernel.

Companion app: MIT. See src/app/LICENSE.
Installer and build tooling: MIT unless a source header states otherwise.

MacKernelSDK is used as a pinned build dependency; its commit, compilation
commands and source hashes are recorded in BUILD.json. Its binaries are not
installed on the target system. The package contains the resulting Intel app
and kernel bundle and their corresponding sources.
